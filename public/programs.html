Science & Technology

Hands-on science for the builders of tomorrow

The pathway focuses on hands-on lab work, robotics, programming, and design thinking. Students build prototypes, write firmware, analyze data, and complete an industry capstone.

Learning Outcomes
Hands-on labsRobotics kits, electronics and sensors.
Coding & firmwarePython, C/C++ and embedded systems.
Prototype designElectromechanical prototyping and testing.
Industry mentorshipCapstone with external partners.
Curriculum Snapshot

  • Foundations of Science
  • Intro to Robotics
  • Mathematics for Engineers
Yr 2
  • Electronics & Circuits
  • Embedded Programming
  • Data & Sensors
Yr 3
  • Advanced Robotics
  • AI & Machine Learning
  • Capstone Project
