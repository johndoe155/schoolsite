Privacy Notice

This notice covers this website — the contact and admissions enquiry forms, and the newsletter. The parent, student and staff portal holds much more data and has its own, longer Privacy Policy. Last updated: 3 October 2026.

In one paragraph: if you send us a message or join the newsletter, we keep what you typed, use it to answer you or to send you updates, and keep it no longer than we need it. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for anything else. You can ask us to delete it at any time and we will.

1. Who is responsible for your data

2. What we collect, and why

  • Contact & admissions forms. Your name, email address and message, plus your IP address and browser user-agent. We use the message to reply to you; we keep the IP address and user-agent so that the form cannot be used to flood our inbox. If you send an enquiry as a parent, it may contain information about your child — please send only what we need to answer you.
  • Newsletter. Your email address, the date you subscribed and the IP address you subscribed from. Used to send school news and to keep the list clean. Every newsletter carries a one-click unsubscribe.
  • Server logs. Short-lived request logs (IP, path, timestamp) kept for security and troubleshooting.

We do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this site, and we set no cookies of our own beyond what is needed to keep the site working. Embedded maps and other third-party content, where present, may set their own cookies under their own policies.

3. Who else sees it

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for marketing. It is accessible only to the staff who need it to answer you, and to the service providers who host the site and deliver our email on our behalf. We may disclose data where the law requires it. We do not transfer it outside Nigeria except to those providers, who process it under contract.

4. How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for 12 months after the last message in the thread, then deleted. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe. Server logs are rotated short. Anything we must keep for a legal reason is kept for the period the law requires and nothing longer.

5. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it, and you may object to how we are using it. Email bicbis95@gmail.com and we will act on the request within the period the law allows. If you are not satisfied with our answer you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

6. Children

This website is not intended for children to use on their own. Enquiries about a pupil should be sent by a parent, guardian or another adult. Pupils’ records are held in the school’s management systems, not on this website.

7. Changes

If this notice changes in a way that affects you, we will update this page and show the new date at the top.
